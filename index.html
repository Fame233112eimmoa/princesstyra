About Tyra

Tyra Maame Serwaa Osei-Wusu

“Some people walk into your life unexpectedly and quietly become the reason your heart feels at home.”

Known to the people closest to her as Sweety, Tyra is more than a name, she is warmth, resilience, kindness, and unwavering determination wrapped into one incredible woman.

Born in Kumasi, Ghana, and now building her future in the United Kingdom, she is a young woman whose story is still being written. Every chapter reflects her courage, her compassion, and her relentless pursuit of becoming the very best version of herself.

This website is a celebration of her life, her dreams, the people she loves, and the beautiful journey we continue to share together.

The Woman She Is

Tyra Maame Serwaa Osei-Wusu is the kind of person whose presence brings peace.

She has a gentle heart, yet she carries the strength of someone who refuses to give up, no matter how difficult life becomes.

She is thoughtful, caring, loving, and incredibly kind. She naturally puts others before herself and has a way of making the people around her feel safe, appreciated, and understood. Her smile has a way of brightening even the hardest days, and her laughter reminds everyone that happiness can be found in life’s simplest moments.

Determination is one of her greatest qualities. Once she decides she wants something, she gives it everything she has until she achieves it. She doesn’t simply dream, she works for those dreams with patience and resilience.

She enjoys the little things that make life beautiful: relaxing with a good movie, especially All American, listening to Joeboy and other great music, challenging herself with mobile and word games, and surrounding herself with everything green.

She absolutely loves dogs and has a funny habit of picking her nose when she thinks nobody is paying attention, a little quirk that somehow makes her even more lovable.

One thing you’ll quickly notice about Tyra is her signature expressions. “I can’t be bothered,” or “Babe, let’s forget about it,” are phrases you’ll hear often, usually accompanied by her adorable habit of squeezing one eye while she talks.

She is wonderfully human, beautiful not because she is perfect, but because every little thing about her feels genuine.

The Beginning of Us

Some of the most life-changing love stories begin in places that seem ordinary at first.

Every love story has a beginning. Ours began on June 5, 2025, when Tyra came to Ghana for her vacation.

The very first moment I saw her at the airport, something changed inside me. Among everyone around us, my eyes found her first. There was something about her that immediately captured my attention. I could not explain it then, but my heart somehow knew she was going to become someone incredibly special.

Not long after, we dropped off our friend Anthony and decided to grab pancakes and juice together. It seemed like such a simple outing, yet it became one of the most unforgettable days of my life. Sometimes the greatest memories begin in the most ordinary places.

One of my favorite memories will always be the night we spent together on the balcony at Pam V Estate. We talked for hours about life, our dreams, our fears, our families, and the future we hoped to build. There was no rush, no distractions, just two hearts learning each other.

That night showed me that love is not always about grand gestures. Sometimes it is simply finding someone you can talk to for hours and still wish the night was longer.

Since that first meeting, Tyra has become my best friend, my safe place, my greatest comfort, and my favorite person. Loving her has become one of the easiest and most beautiful parts of my life.

For Tee

My Forever Letter

If there’s one thing I hope you always remember, it’s this: you are deeply loved.

Hey Tee

You came into my life unexpectedly, yet somehow it feels as though my heart had been waiting for you all along.

Loving you has never been about perfection. It’s about choosing you every single day, even on the ordinary days, even when life becomes difficult, even when the distance feels heavy. I choose you because you are home to me.

I care about your happiness as much as I care about my own. Your victories feel like my victories, and your struggles are burdens I never want you to carry alone. If I could take away every worry that keeps you awake at night, I would do it without hesitation.

One of my biggest fears is losing you not because I doubt us, but because you’ve become such an important part of my life that it’s hard to imagine a future where you aren’t in it.

I want to spend my life supporting your dreams. I want to celebrate every achievement with you, encourage you when you’re tired, remind you of your strength when you doubt yourself, and stand beside you through every season of life.

If life ever asks me to sacrifice my comfort so that you can have more opportunities, more peace, or a brighter future, I would gladly make that choice. Not because love should require losing yourself, but because seeing you grow, smile, and become the incredible midwife you’ve always dreamed of being would bring me genuine joy.

I promise to keep believing in you, even on the days when believing in yourself feels difficult.

I pray that one day we’ll build the home we’ve talked about, fill it with laughter, peace, and love, and continue creating memories that we’ll tell our children and grandchildren about someday.

Thank you for choosing me.

Thank you for trusting me.

Thank you for loving me.

No matter where life takes us, my hope is that we’ll keep choosing each other, again and again.

I love you more than words can fully express, and I’ll never stop appreciating the beautiful woman you are becoming.

Forever yours.
Happy Girlfriend’s Day, and I will be seeing you soon!
